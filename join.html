Find your way in

There’s a place for you here.

Whether you want to volunteer, explore a new field, or take on a role with the core team, start where you are. You don’t need to fit one particular background to be part of the community.

01

Be a volunteer.

Open to people from any background. You could be a high-school student looking to earn volunteer hours, a college student building experience, or simply someone with time, curiosity, and a desire to contribute.

  • No specific academic background required
  • Explore a team and contribute at your pace
  • Receive a certificate on successful completion
  • Eligible volunteers can receive a founder’s letter of recommendation (LOR)

02

Work with the core team.

Want to take on a defined responsibility and help shape the work we do? Explore the open roles and apply to become part of the team.

  • Take ownership of a functional role
  • Collaborate with the core leadership team
  • Help build and grow The Code Society
